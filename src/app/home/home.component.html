BMI calculator — understanding body mass index

The Body Mass Index (BMI) is the globally accepted standard for classifying weight status. Our BMI calculator uses the WHO formula (weight in kg divided by height in metres squared) to instantly categorise you as underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese. Beyond the number, CalculHub explains what your BMI score means for long-term health risks including Type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and hypertension — giving context that raw numbers alone cannot provide. The calculator supports both metric (kg/cm) and imperial (lbs/inches) inputs, making it equally useful for users across India, the US, the UK, and the UAE.

Calorie calculator & TDEE — know your daily energy needs

Whether your goal is fat loss, muscle gain, or maintenance, understanding your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is the single most important dietary metric. Our calorie calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the gold standard recommended by registered dietitians — incorporating your age, gender, height, weight, and activity level to compute your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and adjust it for your daily movement. The result is a precise daily calorie target for your specific goal, alongside a macronutrient breakdown of proteins, carbohydrates, and fats in grams. This is the same methodology used by professional nutritionists and sports scientists.

Ideal weight calculator

Rather than a single target weight, our ideal weight calculator presents a healthy weight range derived from four established medical formulas — Robinson, Miller, Devine, and Hamwi — and provides the range most appropriate for your height and gender. This nuanced approach recognises that ideal body weight is not one-size-fits-all and depends on bone density, muscle mass, and individual body composition. Setting a realistic, evidence-based weight target is the first step to a sustainable fitness journey.

Body fat percentage calculator

BMI tells you about relative weight, but body fat percentage tells you about body composition — a far more meaningful metric for athletes and fitness enthusiasts. Our body fat calculator uses the U.S. Navy circumference method (requiring waist, neck, and hip measurements) to estimate total fat mass versus lean body mass. High body fat — even in individuals with a "normal" BMI, a condition called normal-weight obesity — is associated with elevated metabolic risk. Track your body fat percentage monthly to gauge the real effectiveness of your fitness programme beyond just the weighing scale.

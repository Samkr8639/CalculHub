Hours Calculator — Add Up Work Hours and Calculate Pay

The Hours Calculator (also called a Time Calculator or Work Hours Calculator) helps freelancers, contractors, hourly employees, HR managers, and students quickly add up hours worked across multiple days or shifts. Enter your hours and minutes for each day, and the calculator instantly totals them. Enter an optional hourly rate to automatically calculate your total pay.

This tool is commonly used for timesheet calculations, weekly payroll summaries, project time tracking, and checking overtime eligibility.

Common Use Cases for an Hours Calculator

Freelancers & Contractors

Track billable hours per project per day and calculate your invoice amount at your hourly rate.

HR & Payroll

Calculate total weekly work hours per employee from daily timesheets for salary processing.

Overtime Calculation

In India, any work beyond 9 hours/day (48 hours/week) qualifies for overtime pay at double the rate.

Student Assignments

Track study hours, project time, or internship hours as required by universities or scholarship programs.

Frequently Asked Questions — Hours Calculator

How many hours is a standard work week in India?

Under the Factories Act 1948 and most state labour laws, the standard work week is 48 hours (8 hours/day × 6 days). IT companies often follow a 40-hour, 5-day week. Anything beyond the contracted hours typically requires overtime compensation.

How do I calculate my weekly salary from an hourly rate?

Enter your total working hours per day for each day of the week, enter your hourly rate, and the calculator will multiply total hours by your rate to show your weekly earnings.

Can this calculator handle minutes that exceed 60?

Yes. The calculator converts all time to total minutes first, then converts back to hours and minutes. So 1 hour 75 minutes is correctly displayed as 2 hours 15 minutes.

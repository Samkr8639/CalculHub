What is a Retirement Calculator?

A retirement calculator helps you estimate how large your investment corpus needs to be to maintain your current lifestyle after you stop working, factoring in inflation, life expectancy, and expected investment returns.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current age and desired retirement age.
  2. Input your current monthly expenses and expected inflation rate.
  3. Add your current savings and expected return on investments.
  4. Click Calculate to see your required retirement corpus and whether you are on track.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is inflation important in retirement planning?

Inflation erodes purchasing power. An expense of ₹50,000 today might cost over ₹1.5 Lakhs in 20 years. A good retirement plan must account for the rising cost of living.

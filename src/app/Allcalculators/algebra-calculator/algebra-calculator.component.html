What is an Algebra Calculator?

An algebra calculator is a mathematical tool that solves algebraic equations, simplifies expressions, and performs polynomial operations. It acts as an instant solver for your math homework or engineering problems.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select the operation type (solve equation, simplify, factor, etc.).
  2. Enter your algebraic expression using standard syntax (e.g., 2x + 5 = 15).
  3. Click Calculate to get the step-by-step or final answer.

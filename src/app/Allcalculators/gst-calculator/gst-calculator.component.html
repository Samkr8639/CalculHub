What is a GST Calculator?

A Goods and Services Tax (GST) Calculator helps business owners, consumers, and accountants easily calculate the gross or net price of a product by adding or removing the applicable GST rate.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the net price of the item (or gross price if removing GST).
  2. Select the applicable GST slab (e.g., 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%).
  3. Choose whether to Add GST (find total price) or Remove GST (find base price).
  4. View the calculated CGST, SGST, and Total Amount instantly.

Formula

Add GST: Total = Base Price + (Base Price × GST%)
Remove GST: Base Price = Total / (1 + GST%)

Frequently Asked Questions

What are CGST and SGST?

For intra-state sales, the GST is divided equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). For inter-state sales, Integrated GST (IGST) applies.

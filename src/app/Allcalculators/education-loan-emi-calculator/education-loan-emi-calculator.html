What is an Education Loan EMI Calculator?

This calculator helps students and parents estimate the monthly installments required to repay an education loan. It often accounts for standard repayment terms after the moratorium period ends.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your required education loan amount.
  2. Provide the bank's annual interest rate.
  3. Set the repayment tenure (excluding the study/moratorium period).
  4. Click Calculate to instantly view your repayment schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a moratorium period?

A moratorium is a "repayment holiday" typically spanning the duration of your course plus 6 months or 1 year, during which you are not required to pay EMIs, though simple interest may still accrue.

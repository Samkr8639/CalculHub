What is BMR & TDEE?

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the number of calories your body needs to accomplish its most basic (basal) life-sustaining functions while at rest. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is an estimation of how many calories you burn per day when exercise is taken into account.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your age, gender, height, and weight.
  2. Select your daily activity level from the dropdown.
  3. Click Calculate BMR & TDEE.
  4. Your BMR and daily calorie needs (TDEE) will be displayed.

Formulas

Mifflin-St Jeor Equation:
Men: (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age in years) + 5
Women: (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age in years) - 161
  • TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier (1.2 to 1.9)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is the energy your body burns at rest, while TDEE includes BMR plus the energy used during daily activities and exercise.

How accurate is this calculator?

The Mifflin-St Jeor equation is considered the most accurate formula for the general population, but individual metabolisms can vary.

What is an Investment Calculator?

This calculator estimates the future value of an investment by combining an initial starting balance with regular monthly or annual contributions, growing at an expected rate of return.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your starting investment amount.
  2. Input the amount you plan to contribute regularly.
  3. Set your expected annual return rate and the number of years.
  4. Click Calculate to see your portfolio's projected future value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is regular contribution important?

Regular contributions combined with compound interest drastically accelerate wealth creation. Consistent investing (dollar-cost averaging) also reduces market timing risk.

What is a Home Loan EMI Calculator?

A Home Loan EMI Calculator is an essential financial tool used to calculate your monthly housing loan repayment amount. Because home loans are long-term (up to 30 years), knowing your exact EMI helps you plan your long-term monthly budget effectively.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Input your principal home loan amount.
  2. Enter the current home loan interest rate.
  3. Select your desired loan tenure (in years).
  4. Review the calculated EMI, total interest burden, and the total payment amount over the life of the loan.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ / ((1 + r)ⁿ - 1)

Where P is Principal, r is monthly rate, and n is total number of months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can home loan EMI change over time?

Yes, if you choose a floating interest rate, your EMI or tenure will change whenever the central bank adjusts benchmark repo rates.

Does early repayment reduce the EMI?

Making partial prepayments usually reduces your outstanding principal, which allows you to either lower your future EMIs or reduce your loan tenure.

What is a Mutual Fund Calculator?

This calculator helps investors estimate the returns on their mutual fund investments, either through lump-sum investments or Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), based on historical or expected future returns.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Choose your investment type: Lumpsum or SIP.
  2. Enter your investment amount and the expected rate of return.
  3. Set the time horizon (number of years).
  4. Click Calculate to view your estimated wealth gain and total corpus.

Frequently Asked Questions

What return rate should I expect?

Equity mutual funds historically return between 10-14% annually over the long term, while debt funds return around 6-8%. Returns are not guaranteed and are subject to market risks.
